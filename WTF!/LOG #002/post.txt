Arjun heard about a hidden treasure.

Like everyone else, he wanted it.

The problem?

He had no idea where to find it.

And he wasn't alone.

A doctor.
A teacher.
A shopkeeper.
An engineer.

Everyone wanted the treasure.

Nobody knew the way.

That's when WTF-Bot had an idea:

👉 What if nobody searched alone?

Mutual Fund = Many people investing together while an expert manages the journey.

You don't need to pick every stock yourself.

You don't need to be an expert.

You simply join a team led by one.
