WTF #003 — NAV 📦🤖

Same Basket. New Price.

Arjun found a fruit basket he liked.

🍎 Apple
🥭 Mango
🍊 Orange
🍌 Banana

Price:

₹100

The next day?

₹105

The day after?

₹97

Same basket.

Same fruits.

Different price.

Naturally, Arjun thought:

"Did the shopkeeper change the price again?"

But the answer was surprisingly simple.

📌 The basket didn't change.

📌 The fruits did.

When the value of the things inside changes, the value of the basket changes too.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📖 HUMAN TRANSLATION

NAV = Net Asset Value

Think of a Mutual Fund as a basket.

Think of stocks and other investments as the fruits inside.

When the value of those investments changes, the NAV changes.

That's why the price of a Mutual Fund unit changes every day.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

💡 Beginner Mistake:

Many people think:

NAV ₹10 = Cheap

NAV ₹100 = Expensive

❌ Wrong.

A lower NAV does NOT mean a fund is cheaper or better.

What matters is the quality of the investments inside the fund and how they perform over time.
