⚔️ MONEY & MAHABHARATA — EPISODE 03: ENVY

Duryodhana wasn't poor.
He wasn't powerless.
Yet someone else's success
made him unhappy.

Comparison changes people.

Sometimes it changes spending.
Investing.
Goals.
Peace.

Modern version:
You open Instagram.
Someone bought a house.
Someone doubled money.
Someone is travelling abroad.

Suddenly…

your own progress feels slow.


Question:
What's harder—
Building wealth
or ignoring comparison? 😅
