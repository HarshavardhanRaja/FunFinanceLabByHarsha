⚔️ MONEY & MAHABHARATA — EPISODE 09 ATTACHMENT

One of the most expensive words in investing is:

"Maybe."

Maybe it will recover.

Maybe the business will improve.

Maybe I should wait a little longer.

Bhishma knew what was happening around him.

Yet attachment kept him where he was.

Investors experience the same thing.

Sometimes we don't hold a stock.

The stock holds us.


Question:

What's harder?

Buying a stock...

or admitting it was a mistake? 😅
