EGO

Duryodhana received advice from almost everyone.

He still believed he was right.

That is the danger of ego.

In markets, ego sounds like:

"I know this stock will recover."

"I can't be wrong."

"I'll average more."

Sometimes losses grow…

not because the market changed.

Because the mind refused to.


Question:
What's harder—
Accepting losses
or accepting we were wrong? 😅
