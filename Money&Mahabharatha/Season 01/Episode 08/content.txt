⚔️ MONEY & MAHABHARATA — EPISODE 08 PATIENCE

The Pandavas waited 13 years.

Most investors struggle to wait 13 months.

We live in a world that rewards speed:

⚡ Instant delivery
⚡ Instant entertainment
⚡ Instant opinions

So naturally...

We expect instant wealth too.

A stock doesn't move for 6 months?

We doubt it.

A SIP runs for a year?

We question it.

Returns don't appear immediately?

We change the plan.

But the biggest wealth creators in history had one thing in common:

They gave good decisions enough time to work.

The Pandavas didn't spend exile complaining.

They spent it preparing.

Maybe investing works the same way.


Because compounding has one condition:

It refuses to be rushed.

💬 Tell me:

What's harder for you?
Starting an investment journey...
or staying patient after starting? 😅
