⚔️ MONEY & MAHABHARATA — EPISODE 04 LOYALTY

Karna was admired for loyalty.
But loyalty has a hidden cost.

Many middle-class earners know this feeling:
Helping family.
Supporting friends.
Delaying savings.
Postponing investing.

Years pass.

One day they realise:

"I protected everyone…
except my own future."

Helping people isn't wrong.
Forgetting yourself is.

Question:
What's harder—
Saying YES
or saying NO? 😅
