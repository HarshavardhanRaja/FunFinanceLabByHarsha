IGNORANCE

Abhimanyu was brave.

Bravery wasn't the problem.

Incomplete knowledge was.

Many investors experience something similar:

Buying because everyone talks about it.

Entering because prices rise.

Trusting excitement over understanding.

Markets reward learning.

Not urgency.


Question:

What's riskier—
Not investing
or investing without understanding? 😅
