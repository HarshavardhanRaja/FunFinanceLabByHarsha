⚔️ MONEY & MAHABHARATA — EPISODE 05 DISCIPLINE

Everyone wants long-term wealth.
Few enjoy long-term discipline.

Investing sounds exciting until:
SIPs continue for years.
Returns come slowly.
Nothing dramatic happens.
That is where many stop.

Krishna spoke often about duty.
Doing what must be done.
Even when rewards aren't immediate.

Maybe wealth works similarly.

Question:
What's harder—
Starting
or staying consistent? 😅
