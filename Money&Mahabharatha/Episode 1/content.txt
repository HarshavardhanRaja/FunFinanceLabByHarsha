Arjuna stood on the battlefield and froze.

Many investors do the same.

Not during war.

During market crashes. 📉

Portfolio down 10%:

Investor:
“I believe in long term investing.”

Portfolio down 20%:

Investor:
“Maybe FD wasn't a bad idea…”

Fear changes decisions faster than data.

One lesson from this episode stayed with me:

You don't need to remove fear.

You need to stop fear from driving the decision.

Because markets recover.

Panic often doesn't.
