## 🎯 Typical Episode Workflow

```
1. Open episode folder
   ↓
2. Fill in idea.md
   └─ Hook, concept, key points, visuals
   ↓
3. Write script.md
   └─ Full dialogue with timing
   ↓
4. Add video assets to video/
   └─ Raw clips, b-roll, etc.
   ↓
5. Record/Edit episode
   ↓
6. Update README.md with links
   ├─ YouTube URL
   ├─ Reels/Shorts URLs
   └─ Status: Published
   ↓
7. Add metrics to PUBLISHING_TRACKER.md
   └─ Views, likes, comments, etc.
   ↓
8. Move to next episode!
```
