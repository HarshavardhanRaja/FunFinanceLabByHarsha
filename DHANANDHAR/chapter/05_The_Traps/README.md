# 🎯 Purpose of Chapter V

The purpose of **The Traps** is NOT to teach:

❌ Investing strategies  
❌ Wealth creation  
❌ Spending habits  
❌ Portfolio building  

The purpose is much simpler:

> Teach people how to avoid financial destruction.

This chapter should make viewers understand:

- High returns often hide high risk
- Scams evolve faster than awareness
- Emotions destroy money
- Crowds are frequently wrong
- Bad advice can become expensive

At the end of this chapter, the audience should think:

> **"Avoiding mistakes may be more important than chasing returns."**

If they feel this…

The Traps worked.

---

# ⚠️ Important Difference:
# THE LEAK 💧 vs THE TRAPS ⚠️

### THE LEAK 💧

Slow money loss.

Examples:

- Lifestyle inflation
- Overspending
- EMI dependency
- Social pressure spending

Feels like:

```text
"Where is my money going?"
```

---

### THE TRAPS ⚠️

Sudden wealth destruction.

Examples:

- Scams
- FOMO investing
- Fraud
- Loan traps
- Bad advice

Feels like:

```text
"How did I lose so much?"
```

---

Simple rule:

```text
Leak = habits

Trap = mistakes
```
