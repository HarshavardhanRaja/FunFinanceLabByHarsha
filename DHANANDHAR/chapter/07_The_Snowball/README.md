# 🎯 Purpose of Chapter VII

The purpose of **The Snowball** is NOT to teach:

❌ Stock picking  
❌ Investment options  
❌ Trading  
❌ Quick returns  

The purpose is much simpler:

> Teach people how wealth compounds over long periods.

This chapter should make viewers understand:

- Wealth is usually boring
- Time is often more powerful than intelligence
- Systems outperform excitement
- Staying invested matters

At the end of this chapter, the audience should think:

> **"Maybe building wealth is slower… but simpler… than I imagined."**

If they feel this…

The Snowball worked.
