# 🎯 Purpose of Chapter II

The purpose of **The Shield** is NOT to teach:

❌ Stocks  
❌ Mutual Funds  
❌ Wealth Building  
❌ Portfolio Creation  

The purpose is much simpler:

> Teach people how NOT to get financially destroyed.

This chapter should make viewers understand:

- One hospital bill can erase years of savings
- One emergency can force debt
- One missing insurance policy can impact an entire family
- Protection is not optional

At the end of this chapter, the audience should think:

> **"I need to protect my finances before trying to grow them."**

If they feel this…

The Shield worked.
