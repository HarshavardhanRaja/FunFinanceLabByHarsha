# 🎯 Purpose of Chapter IV

The purpose of **The Leak** is NOT to teach:

❌ Investing  
❌ Wealth building  
❌ Stock markets  
❌ Retirement planning  

The purpose is much simpler:

> Teach people how money quietly disappears through everyday behaviour.

This chapter should make viewers understand:

- Higher income doesn't automatically create wealth
- Lifestyle upgrades can become traps
- Spending deserves strategy
- Small decisions repeated over years matter

At the end of this chapter, the audience should think:

> **"Maybe my money isn't missing… maybe it's leaking."**

If they feel this…

The Leak worked.
