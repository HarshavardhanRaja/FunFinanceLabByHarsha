# 🎯 Purpose of Chapter I

The purpose of **The Spark** is NOT to teach:

❌ Investing  
❌ Stocks  
❌ Mutual Funds  
❌ Budgeting  

The purpose is much simpler:

> Make people uncomfortable with their current financial reality.

This chapter should make viewers question:

- The traditional life path
- The salary → EMI loop
- Their understanding of wealth
- Their relationship with money

At the end of this chapter, the audience should think:

> **"Maybe I've been playing the wrong financial game."**

If they feel this…

The Spark worked.
