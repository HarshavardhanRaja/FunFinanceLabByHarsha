# 🎯 Purpose of Chapter VI

The purpose of **The Compass** is NOT to teach:

❌ Advanced investing  
❌ Portfolio construction  
❌ Wealth optimization  
❌ Stock picking  

The purpose is much simpler:

> Teach people what investment options exist, how they differ, and who they may suit.

This chapter should make viewers understand:

- Investing ≠ only stocks
- Every investment has tradeoffs
- Risk and return are connected
- Choosing matters more than copying

At the end of this chapter, the audience should think:

> **"I finally understand where my money can go."**

If they feel this…

The Compass worked.
