# 🎯 Purpose of Chapter VIII

The purpose of **The Crossroads** is NOT to teach:

❌ Investing options  
❌ Wealth compounding  
❌ Stock markets  
❌ Budgeting  

The purpose is much simpler:

> Teach people how major life decisions shape financial outcomes.

This chapter should make viewers understand:

- Marriage affects money
- Housing decisions affect freedom
- Kids affect long-term planning
- Retirement starts earlier than expected
- Family responsibilities need strategy

At the end of this chapter, the audience should think:

> **"Life decisions may matter more than investment decisions."**

If they feel this…

The Crossroads worked.
