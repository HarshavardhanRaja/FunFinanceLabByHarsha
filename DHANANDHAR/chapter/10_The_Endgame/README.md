# 🎯 Purpose of Chapter X

The purpose of **The Endgame** is NOT to teach:

❌ Investing  
❌ Budgeting  
❌ Wealth accumulation  
❌ Income growth  

The purpose is much simpler:

> Explore what comes after wealth: freedom, meaning, choice and legacy.

This chapter should make viewers understand:

- Wealth is a tool
- Financial freedom looks different for everyone
- Legacy matters
- More money does not automatically mean more happiness

At the end of this chapter, the audience should think:

> **"What kind of life am I actually trying to build?"**

If they feel this…

The Endgame worked.
