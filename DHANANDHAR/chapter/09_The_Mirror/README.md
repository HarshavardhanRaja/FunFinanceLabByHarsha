# 🎯 Purpose of Chapter IX

The purpose of **The Mirror** is NOT to teach:

❌ Investing  
❌ Budgeting  
❌ Portfolio building  
❌ Wealth systems  

The purpose is much simpler:

> Teach people how emotions, habits, identity and beliefs shape money outcomes.

This chapter should make viewers understand:

- Behaviour affects wealth
- Emotions influence decisions
- Discipline often beats intelligence
- Childhood beliefs quietly shape adulthood

At the end of this chapter, the audience should think:

> **"Maybe my biggest financial obstacle isn't money… maybe it's behaviour."**

If they feel this…

The Mirror worked.
